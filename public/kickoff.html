6. Project Terms, Acceptance & Signatures

Key Implementation Terms

  • The implementation scope will be governed by the approved proposal, purchase order and this kick-off document.
  • The customer will provide required data, infrastructure, approvals and user availability within the agreed timeline.
  • Existing standard modules will be implemented first. New customisation, workflow, report or feature requests will follow a separate change-request and commercial approval process.
  • Training will be conducted as per the agreed schedule. Customer users are responsible for attending training and completing practical working.
  • Delays caused by data, infrastructure, user unavailability or approval dependencies may result in a revised project timeline.
  • The client is requested to ensure that the scheduled training sessions are attended as planned to support timely implementation. In case additional training days are required due to delays in attending the scheduled sessions, the applicable additional training charges, as mentioned in the sales proposal, may apply, unless otherwise agreed upon in writing by Indas Analytics.
  • After completion of module training and go-live activities, the project will proceed to formal implementation sign-off.
  • After sign-off, the project will be handed over to the INDAS support team. Support will be provided through phone, email and priority-based assistance.
  • The assigned implementation engineer may be changed when required. INDAS will communicate the change to maintain project continuity.

Kick-off Acceptance Declaration

By signing this document, both parties confirm that the project scope, responsibilities, implementation approach, module owners, proposed timeline, dependencies and communication process have been discussed and understood. Both parties agree to cooperate for the timely implementation and go-live of INDAS ERP. Any change outside the agreed scope will be handled through mutual written approval.
