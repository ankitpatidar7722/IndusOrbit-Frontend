7. Customer Declaration & Go-Live Acceptance

1The ERP modules selected in the approved implementation scope have been configured and demonstrated by Indas Analytics.
2The customer’s nominated users have attended the required training sessions and are capable of operating the ERP modules assigned to them.
3Practical working sessions have been completed and users have been given sufficient opportunity to perform applicable transactions.
4Training recordings and agreed reference materials have been shared with the customer for future use.
5The customer has reviewed and received the agreed deliverables.
6Queries raised during implementation have been addressed or formally recorded in the Pending Items Register.
7The customer accepts INDAS ERP for live business operations from the agreed go-live date and assumes responsibility for timely and accurate day-to-day data entry by its users.
8Any future enhancement, additional development, custom report, new workflow, process change, integration or requirement outside the approved implementation scope shall be treated as a separate requirement and may be estimated, approved and scheduled independently.
9This sign-off records operational completion and does not replace the terms of any signed commercial agreement, proposal, purchase order or statement of work between the parties.

Operational Responsibility After Go-Live

Customer users will enter transactions accurately and on time in the assigned ERP modules.
Customer management will ensure user availability, process discipline and internal ownership.
Customer will maintain appropriate user access and promptly report employee / role changes.
Indas Analytics will provide support for reported issues through the communicated support channels.
Enhancements or scope additions will be handled through a separate requirement and approval process.

8. Support & Operational Handover
